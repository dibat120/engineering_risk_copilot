Engineering risk is the possibility that a system, process, project, or design may fail to achieve its intended technical, safety, cost, schedule, or performance objectives.

Risk is commonly evaluated by considering both the likelihood of an unwanted event and the severity of its consequences.

A simple qualitative risk assessment may classify risk as low, medium, or high based on probability and consequence.

Risk mitigation aims to reduce either the likelihood of the event, the severity of the consequence, or both.

Engineering risk management is an iterative process involving identification, analysis, evaluation, treatment, monitoring, and communication.
